## Assignment Scope

HW01 is a warm-up assignment. It does not use the EShop software system. The work focuses on:

* QA/QC job-market research
* Public software defect analysis
* Test-case design and execution for one real physical product
* AI collaboration, audit, critique, and disclosure

## Key Constraints

* Job postings must be published within 60 days of submission
* At least 3 job postings must require AI, LLM, or automation-AI skills
* Software defects must be publicized between 2022 and 2026
* At least 5 software defects must be AI/LLM related
* The physical product must be a specific device owned by the student
* Device photo must show the device and student ID card in the same frame
* At least 5 test cases must be executed on the real device
* At least 5 execution videos must be recorded with student voice narration
* At least 3 test cases must be edge cases that AI did not find
* AI use must be disclosed and audited

---

## Anti-Cheat Evidence

Artifacts that must be produced by the student, not AI:

* Device photo with student ID card
* Execution videos with student voice narration
* Job-posting screenshots showing account/login name
* Prompt log entries with timestamps
* Real GitHub issue evidence for physical-device defects

## Repository Role

The repository should show:

* Iterative progress
* Clear commits

## AI Use Position

AI may be used for:

* Brainstorming test ideas
* Drafting first-pass analyses
* Reviewing structure
* Comparing alternatives
* Finding possible missing edge cases
* Preparing audit and critique drafts

AI must not be treated as final authority. Facts, evidence, requirements, defect claims, and final judgments must be manually checked.

---

## Final Notes

When prompting AI, include this context or a concise summary of it. Many AI mistakes come from missing assignment constraints, especially evidence and anti-cheat requirements.
