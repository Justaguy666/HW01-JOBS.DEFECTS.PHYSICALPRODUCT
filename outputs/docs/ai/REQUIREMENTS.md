## Requirement 1 - QA/QC Job Market 2026+

Required:

* Find 10 QA/QC job postings
* Postings must be published within 60 days of submission date
* At least 3 postings must require AI, LLM, automation-AI, or related skills
* Each posting needs a link
* Each posting needs a dated screenshot
* Each screenshot must show account/login name
* Each posting needs job description, required skills, and salary
* Each posting needs AI Impact Analysis


## Requirement 3 - Physical Product Testing

Required:

* Choose one specific household physical device
* Submit one photo showing the device and student ID card in the same frame
* Declare brand, model, year, and serial number
* Mask the middle 4 characters of the serial number
* Design 15 test cases
* Execute at least 5 test cases on the real device
* Record short videos for at least 5 executed test cases
* Videos should be 60 seconds or less
* Videos must contain student voice narration
* Aim to find at least 5 defects
* At least 3 test cases must be AI-missed edge cases
* For AI-missed edge cases, provide AI conversation screenshot and written explanation

Recommended artifact IDs:

```text
DEV-001
TC-001 through TC-015
EV-XXX for photo, video notes, screenshots
```
