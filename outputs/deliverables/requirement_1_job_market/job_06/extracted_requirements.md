# Extracted Requirements - Job 06

## Role Summary

The role requires a Senior Manual Test Engineer with strong hands-on testing experience, Agile collaboration, mentoring ability, and optional automation or AI-assisted development capabilities.

## Testing Responsibilities

- Perform testing activities to improve product quality and support product success.
- Improve test coverage and testing effectiveness through creativity and initiative.
- Analyze test needs methodically with detail-oriented problem solving.
- Support product growth activities such as competitor research and customer troubleshooting.
- Participate in sprint planning and provide QA/test recommendations.
- Collaborate with clients and resolve testing issues proactively.

## Technical Skills

- Testing web-based applications.
- Test strategy, test approach, test planning, black-box testing, risk-based testing, exploratory testing, and non-UI testing.
- Strong understanding of SDLC and STLC.
- Automation experience with tools such as Katalon, Selenium, Robotium, UiAutomator, XCTest, or XCUITest is a plus.
- Experience with TestNG, Mocha, Jasmine, Nightwatch, or similar tools is a plus.

## Experience and Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Information Technology, or a related field.
- 5+ years of hands-on software testing experience.
- Intermediate English proficiency and strong communication skills.
- Experience mentoring, coaching, and sharing testing best practices.
- Ability to work independently and lead testing activities within a team.
- Familiarity with Agile development methodologies.

## AI-Related Nice-to-Have Requirements

- Daily use of AI coding tools such as Copilot, Cursor, or Claude Code across the SDLC.
- Ability to decompose complex tasks and provide effective AI context.
- Experience with multi-step prompting, agentic workflows, AI-driven development frameworks, or MCP-style tool integration.
- Awareness of emerging AI tools and willingness to evaluate new capabilities.
