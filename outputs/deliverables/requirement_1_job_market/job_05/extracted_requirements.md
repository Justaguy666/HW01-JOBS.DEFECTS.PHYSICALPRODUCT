# Extracted Requirements - Job 05

## Role Summary

The role requires a Lead QA/QC Tester who combines team leadership with hands-on automation strategy, Playwright/Python implementation, test reporting, and AI-assisted testing across the full lifecycle.

## Leadership Responsibilities

- Lead, mentor, and guide up to five QA Engineers.
- Build a collaborative and high-performing QA team environment.
- Provide technical guidance, support team growth, delegate tasks, and balance workload.

## Testing Responsibilities

- Analyze product requirements and design documents to improve documentation quality and testability.
- Own and improve the integration testing strategy.
- Review test scenarios, test cases, and test scripts for coverage and accuracy.
- Design, develop, and maintain scalable automation frameworks and scripts using Playwright with Python.
- Perform hybrid manual and automation testing.
- Support CI/CD practices for automated tests and conduct performance testing.
- Monitor, analyze, visualize, and report test results.
- Collaborate with development teams, product managers, and other stakeholders.

## Technical Skills

- Strong automation testing experience.
- Familiarity with Playwright Python; Playwright TypeScript is a plus.
- Ability to apply AI-driven solutions across the end-to-end testing lifecycle.
- Good English communication and documentation skills.

## Experience and Qualifications

- 7+ years of experience with a focus on automation testing.
- ISTQB or equivalent testing certification.
- Experience with OutSystems or low-code/no-code platforms is a plus.
