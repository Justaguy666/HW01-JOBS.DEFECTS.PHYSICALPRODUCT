### Job description

**This position will be contracted through Bosch’s external vendor under a one-year agreement.**

**Team Leadership & Mentorship (30%):**

- Lead, mentor, and guide a team of up to 5 Quality Assurance Engineers.
- Foster a collaborative and high-performing team environment.
- Provide technical guidance and support to team members, helping them grow their skills and careers.
- Delegate tasks effectively and ensure equitable workload distribution.

**Test Strategy & Execution (70%):**

- Product Requirement: Analyze product requirements and design documents, contributing expertise to ensure comprehensive and accurate documentation.
- Test Strategy: Take ownership of, and continuously improve, our existing integration testing strategy.
- Test Case: Conduct comprehensive reviews of test scenarios, test cases, and test scripts to ensure thorough coverage, accuracy, and adherence to best practices.
- Test Execution: Design, develop, and maintain robust and scalable automation frameworks and scripts using Playwright with Python, along with manual testing as a hybrid Tester; drive continuous integration and continuous delivery (CI/CD) practices for automated tests; conduct performance tests.
- Test Monitoring & Report: Monitor, analyze, and report on test results, identifying trends and areas for improvement, provide visualization of results using graphical tools.
- Team collaboration: closely with development teams, product managers, and other stakeholders to understand requirements and ensure testability.
- AI leverage: Ability to apply AI-driven solutions across the end‑to‑end testing lifecycle to improve efficiency, coverage, ...
