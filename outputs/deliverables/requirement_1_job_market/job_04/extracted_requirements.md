# Extracted Requirements - Job 04

## Role Summary

The role requires a Senior Automation Tester who can build scalable automation frameworks using Playwright and Python for enterprise workflow systems and CI/CD quality gates.

## Testing Responsibilities

- Design, develop, and maintain automation test frameworks using Playwright with Python.
- Perform both automation and manual testing activities.
- Review product requirements, test scenarios, and test cases for quality coverage.
- Integrate automated tests into CI/CD pipelines.
- Monitor test results, analyze issues, and propose quality improvements.
- Collaborate with developers, product managers, and global stakeholders.
- Mentor team members on automation testing practices.

## Technical Skills

- Strong hands-on experience with Playwright and Python.
- Good understanding of CI/CD and modern testing practices.
- Ability to design comprehensive test frameworks and support shift-left testing.
- Good English communication skills.

## Experience and Qualifications

- 5+ years of software testing experience with a strong focus on automation testing.
- Strong analytical and problem-solving mindset.
- Ability to work on large-scale solutions and quality infrastructure for global users.

## Nice-to-Have Requirements

- Experience with low-code or no-code platforms.
- ISTQB certification.
- Experience with performance testing or AI-supported testing solutions.
