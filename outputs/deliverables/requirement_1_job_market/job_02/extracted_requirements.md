# Extracted Requirements - Job 02

## Role Summary

The role requires a QC Executive for software quality control in a retail/business system environment, with emphasis on test planning, defect tracking, ERP testing, and collaboration with product and engineering teams.

## Testing Responsibilities

- Create test plans and detailed test cases for developed software.
- Execute testing, log defects, and follow up on bug-fixing progress with the development team.
- Track the defect lifecycle and monitor quality issues that may arise in the system.
- Ensure the delivered system or product matches the agreed design and requirements.
- Coordinate with backend, web, and mobile engineering teams to maintain consistent product quality.
- Work with product design or product development teams to clarify and complete detailed requirements.

## Technical Skills

- Software testing knowledge across functional, regression, integration, and API testing.
- Ability to analyze logic, predict edge cases, and identify scenarios that may cause system errors.
- Basic or practical knowledge of using AI tools to optimize test case writing, mock data generation, or test script support.
- Automation testing with TypeScript, Playwright for web, and Appium for mobile is a strong advantage.

## Experience and Domain Knowledge

- Practical experience testing ERP systems or similarly complex enterprise management systems.
- Understanding of business workflows and system behavior in retail or enterprise software contexts.
- Ability to work proactively with minimal supervision.

## Communication and Process Requirements

- Write clear bug reports and communicate effectively with developers, product members, and QA stakeholders.
- Listen, clarify, and transfer information accurately between teams.
- Keep learning new testing trends, tools, and processes to improve work efficiency.
