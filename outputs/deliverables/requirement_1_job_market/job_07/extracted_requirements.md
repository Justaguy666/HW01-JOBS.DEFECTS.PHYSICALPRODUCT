# Extracted Requirements - Job 07

## Role Summary

The role requires a Principal QC Engineer who owns organizational QC strategy, drives consistent quality practices across multiple squads, and promotes AI-powered tools and automation to reduce manual effort.

## Leadership and Strategy Responsibilities

- Define and implement QC strategy across six development squads.
- Champion a quality-first engineering culture.
- Establish and maintain QC standards, testing processes, and best practices.
- Lead initiatives to improve QC capability, automation adoption, output, and product quality.
- Mentor QC members and support team development across squads.

## Testing and Quality Responsibilities

- Oversee functional, integration, system, security, and performance test coverage strategy.
- Ensure consistent defect management and testing standards across teams.
- Identify systemic quality risks and drive improvement initiatives.
- Consolidate testing results, defect trends, and coverage data.
- Provide quality insights and release-readiness reporting to leadership.

## Technical Skills

- Strong knowledge of test strategy, defect management, quality processes, and test automation frameworks.
- Experience with CI/CD tools and shift-left testing concepts.
- Knowledge of API testing, microservices, regression testing, and SQL or database validation.
- Experience using AI-assisted tools to improve QC outcomes.

## Experience and Qualifications

- 7+ years of QA/QC experience.
- Experience leading or mentoring QA/QC teams in Agile environments.
- Strong communication, leadership, cross-team collaboration, and English communication skills.
- ISTQB certification is a plus.
