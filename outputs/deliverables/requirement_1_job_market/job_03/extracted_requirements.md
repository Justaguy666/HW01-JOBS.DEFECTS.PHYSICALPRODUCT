# Extracted Requirements - Job 03

## Role Summary

The role requires an Automation QA Engineer who can design, build, and execute automated tests while collaborating with developers and QA teams to validate software quality.

## Testing Responsibilities

- Understand project requirements and product functionality with developers and QA team members.
- Design, develop, execute, and manage automated test scripts.
- Identify and report software defects clearly and concisely.
- Analyze test results, troubleshoot issues, and support continuous improvement of automation testing processes.
- Use AI technologies to design, create, and manage automation test cases.

## Technical Skills

- Proficiency in at least one programming language such as Java, Python, or C#.
- Familiarity with automation tools such as Selenium, Appium, or equivalent tools.
- Basic knowledge of version control systems such as Git.
- Understanding of SDLC, testing methodologies, and automation frameworks.

## Experience and Qualifications

- Bachelor's degree in Computer Science, Engineering, or a related field.
- 3+ years of relevant experience.
- Strong analytical, problem-solving, communication, and collaboration skills.

## Nice-to-Have Requirements

- Experience with JUnit, TestNG, Selenium WebDriver, or similar test automation frameworks.
- Understanding of HTML, CSS, and JavaScript.
- API testing experience using tools such as Postman or RestAssured.
- Knowledge of performance testing concepts and tools.
- Experience with VMware, Hyper-V, AWS, cloud storage, and AI automation tools such as Copilot, Cursor, or Perplexity.
