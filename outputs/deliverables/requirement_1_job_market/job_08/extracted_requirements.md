# Extracted Requirements - Job 08

## Role Summary

The role requires a Process Quality Assurance specialist who controls process compliance, audits project execution, prepares quality reports, and supports final quality gates before customer delivery.

## Quality Responsibilities

- Control project process compliance during product development.
- Perform process audits and support common quality programs from the Fsoft Quality Center.
- Coordinate with project teams to create quality-related reports.
- Report project status to Project Managers and Quality Assurance Managers.
- Participate in product quality assessment and review quality reports before customer submission.
- Support final inspection and quality gate checks before product delivery.

## Technical and Process Skills

- Data analysis, synthesis, and reporting ability.
- Knowledge of software or project processes.
- Excel skills.
- Knowledge of CMMI.
- Ability to communicate with multiple stakeholders and promote process compliance.

## Experience and Qualifications

- Graduate from an IT or foreign language university.
- Agile, enthusiastic, eager to learn, and able to work under pressure.
- Foreign language capability: IELTS 7.0 or equivalent TOEIC, or Japanese N2.
