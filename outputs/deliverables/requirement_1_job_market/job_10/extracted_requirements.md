# Extracted Requirements - Job 10

## Role Summary

The role requires a Software Tester for web and mobile software products, covering requirement analysis, test case design, functional/non-functional testing, automation, defect reporting, and process improvement.

## Testing Responsibilities

- Participate in software development projects and test web/mobile products.
- Analyze requirements and design test cases and test scenarios.
- Perform functional testing, performance testing, and security testing.
- Execute automation testing using suitable tools and frameworks.
- Record, analyze, track, and report discovered defects.
- Coordinate with team members to ensure product quality.
- Contribute to improving testing processes and product quality.

## Technical Skills

- Knowledge of software testing processes, test types, and testing techniques.
- Experience with tools such as Selenium, JUnit, TestNG, JMeter, and Postman.
- Ability to read technical documents and business requirements.
- Database experience with MySQL, SQL Server, Oracle, or similar systems.
- Project management tools such as Jira or Trello.
- Programming language experience such as Java or Python is an advantage.

## Experience and Qualifications

- 1-3 years of relevant experience; fresher candidates under 1 year may also be accepted.
- Good analytical, problem-solving, and logical thinking skills.
- Ability to work independently and in a team.
- Good communication, presentation, and test reporting skills.
- ISTQB or related testing certification is preferred.
