# Extracted Requirements - Job 01

## Role Summary

The role requires a QA Engineer with strong manual, exploratory, integration, and system-level testing skills, combined with an AI-first mindset for improving QA productivity and coverage.

## Testing Responsibilities

- Perform manual, exploratory, integration, regression, API, and system-level testing.
- Validate frontend, backend, APIs, databases, integrations, and production-like workflows.
- Investigate defects, analyze root causes, and monitor release quality indicators.
- Develop and maintain automated tests for regression, smoke, API, and integration testing.
- Support CI/CD testing workflows and contribute to performance, reliability, stress, and scalability testing.

## Technical Skills

- Automation tools such as Selenium, Playwright, Cypress, or Robot Framework.
- Scripting or programming ability in JavaScript, Java, or Python.
- API testing and backend validation.
- Familiarity with test management and defect tracking tools such as Jira, qTest, Xray, or TestRail.
- Familiarity with Agile/Scrum and CI/CD environments.

## AI-Related Requirements

- Use AI or Agentic AI tools to generate, validate, maintain, and optimize test plans, test cases, and regression suites.
- Review AI-generated scenarios, scripts, and outputs for accuracy, completeness, and business relevance.
- Apply AI-assisted workflows to defect analysis, root-cause investigation, and test coverage improvement.
- Evaluate emerging AI testing tools and QA productivity solutions.

## Experience and Qualifications

- 4+ years of software testing experience with strong manual testing expertise.
- Experience testing complex, distributed, high-traffic, or highly integrated systems.
- Strong analytical thinking, troubleshooting, communication, and independent working ability.
- Preferred experience with embedded systems, IoT, industrial software, Linux, shell scripting, microservices, event-driven systems, or performance testing tools.
