## 2. Defect Description

```text
After rapid but non-forced open-close handling, the right hinge developed a light clicking sound and felt looser than the left hinge.
```

---

## 3. Steps To Reproduce

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Inspect both hinges before the test and confirm no obvious looseness. |
| 2 | Open and close the foldable hinge 20 times at a faster-than-normal but non-forced pace. |
| 3 | Compare left and right hinge resistance after the cycles. |
| 4 | Power on the headset and confirm whether audio still works. |

---

## 4. Expected Result

```text
Both hinges should remain physically stable and symmetric after repeated normal handling, with no new clicking, looseness, or alignment change.
```

---

## 5. Actual Result

```text
The right hinge clicked lightly after the rapid cycles and felt looser than the left side. Audio still worked, but the mechanical feel changed.
```

---

## 6. Impact And Severity Rationale

```text
This is a durability concern because repeated folding is expected for a portable headset. Severity is medium because function still works, but the symptom may worsen with continued use.
```

---

## 7. Mitigation Or Follow-Up

```text
Avoid rapid repeated folding and monitor the hinge during future use. Follow-up should repeat a lower-speed fold test after 24 hours and check whether looseness increases.
```
