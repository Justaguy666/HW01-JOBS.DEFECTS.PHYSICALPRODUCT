## 2. Defect Description

```text
After the headset was power-cycled, the previously paired Android phone did not reconnect automatically within a reasonable waiting period. The user had to open Bluetooth settings and manually select the headset again.
```

---

## 3. Steps To Reproduce

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Pair the BRIDIO TH10 with the Android phone and confirm audio playback. |
| 2 | Power off the headset, wait 30 seconds, then power it on again. |
| 3 | Leave the phone Bluetooth enabled and wait up to 60 seconds for automatic reconnection. |
| 4 | Attempt playback and record whether manual selection is required. |

---

## 4. Expected Result

```text
The headset should reconnect automatically to the previously paired phone, or recover with one simple tap without requiring re-pairing or repeated manual steps.
```

---

## 5. Actual Result

```text
The headset did not reconnect automatically within 60 seconds in 2 of 3 attempts. Manual selection from Bluetooth settings was required, and the first playback attempt had about 2 seconds of silence.
```

---

## 6. Impact And Severity Rationale

```text
This affects daily usability because users expect a paired headset to recover after normal power cycling. The issue is not safety-critical, but it creates repeated setup friction and may be mistaken for a pairing failure.
```

---

## 7. Mitigation Or Follow-Up

```text
Workaround is to manually select the headset in Bluetooth settings after power-on. Follow-up retest should include clearing the pairing record, re-pairing, and repeating at least five power-cycle attempts.
```
