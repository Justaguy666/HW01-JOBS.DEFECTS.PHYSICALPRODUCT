## 2. Defect Description

```text
Playback became unstable when a common household obstruction was placed between the source phone and the headset, even though the distance was below the claimed approximate 10 m Bluetooth range.
```

---

## 3. Steps To Reproduce

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Connect the headset to the Android phone and start music playback in line of sight. |
| 2 | Move the phone behind a closed wooden door and one wall at approximately 4.7 meters. |
| 3 | Continue playback for 2 minutes without changing volume or touching the headset. |
| 4 | Move back toward the door and observe whether playback recovers. |

---

## 4. Expected Result

```text
Minor degradation may occur with an obstruction, but playback should remain usable and should not repeatedly drop at a short household distance.
```

---

## 5. Actual Result

```text
Audio dropped every 10-20 seconds behind the obstruction. The connection did not fully disconnect, but the listening experience was interrupted until the source device was moved closer.
```

---

## 6. Impact And Severity Rationale

```text
This limits practical use when the phone is left in another room. The severity is medium because the core Bluetooth function still works in open space but degrades in a common indoor scenario.
```

---

## 7. Mitigation Or Follow-Up

```text
Recommended workaround is keeping the source device in the same room. Follow-up should compare behavior with another phone and repeat the test through a lighter obstruction.
```
