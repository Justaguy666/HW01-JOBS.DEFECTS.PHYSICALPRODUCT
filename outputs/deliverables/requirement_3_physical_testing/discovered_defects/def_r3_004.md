## 2. Defect Description

```text
The headset microphone produced a low-volume, muffled recording in a quiet room. The same phrase recorded with the phone microphone was clearer under the same conditions.
```

---

## 3. Steps To Reproduce

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Connect the BRIDIO TH10 to the phone by Bluetooth. |
| 2 | Open the phone voice recorder in a quiet room. |
| 3 | Record a 20-second spoken phrase through the headset microphone. |
| 4 | Record the same phrase with the phone microphone and compare clarity. |

---

## 4. Expected Result

```text
The headset microphone should capture speech clearly enough for calls or voice messages in a quiet environment.
```

---

## 5. Actual Result

```text
The headset microphone recording was low, muffled, and slightly noisy. Several words required replay to understand clearly, while the phone microphone version was clear.
```

---

## 6. Impact And Severity Rationale

```text
This impacts calls, online meetings, and voice messages. Severity is medium because microphone quality is a core headset function, although playback is unaffected.
```

---

## 7. Mitigation Or Follow-Up

```text
Use the phone microphone for important calls when possible. Follow-up retest should include another recording app and a call test with the receiver rating speech clarity.
```
