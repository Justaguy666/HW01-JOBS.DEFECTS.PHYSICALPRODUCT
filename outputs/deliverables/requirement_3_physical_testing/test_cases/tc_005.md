## 2. Objective

Check Bluetooth behavior when a typical household obstruction exists between the phone and headset.

---

## 3. Preconditions

* Headset is connected to a phone or laptop by Bluetooth.
* Audio is playing continuously.
* A safe household wall or closed door can be used as the obstruction.

---

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Device under test | BRIDIO TH10 |

---

## 5. Steps

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Start audio playback in line of sight. |
| 2 | Move behind one wall or closed door. |
| 3 | Continue listening for 2 minutes. |
| 4 | Return to line of sight. |
| 5 | Record whether audio recovers if degradation occurs. |

---

## 6. Expected Result

Audio behavior is observable and recoverable. Minor degradation may occur, but the headset should not require a full reset after returning closer.

---

## 7. Actual Result

```text
Recorded 2026-06-01 21:31 ICT. With a closed wooden door and one wall between the phone and headset at about 4.7 meters, playback had repeated short dropouts every 10-20 seconds. Moving back closer to the door restored stable audio without a full reset.
```
