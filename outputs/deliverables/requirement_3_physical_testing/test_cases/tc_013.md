## 2. Objective

Verify whether the foldable hinge or internal wiring causes audio interruption while the headset is playing sound.

---

## 3. Preconditions

* Headset is connected by Bluetooth.
* Continuous audio is playing at medium volume.
* Tester uses normal hand force only.
* No excessive bending or unsafe force is applied.

---

## 4. Test Data And Inputs

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Device under test | BRIDIO TH10 |
| Playback mode | Bluetooth music playback |
| Mechanical action | Slow fold/unfold motion |
| Safety constraint | Normal careful hand force only |

---

## 5. Steps

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Start Bluetooth music playback at medium volume. |
| 2 | Slowly fold one hinge partway while keeping playback active. |
| 3 | Unfold it back to normal position. |
| 4 | Repeat for the other side. |
| 5 | Record any audio cut, crackle, channel loss, or Bluetooth disconnect. |

---

## 6. Expected Result

Audio should continue without crackle, one-side channel loss, or disconnection during normal careful folding/unfolding movement.

---

## 7. Actual Result

```text
Recorded 2026-06-01 22:24 ICT. Slow fold/unfold movement on both sides while music was playing did not interrupt Bluetooth playback. No one-side channel loss or disconnect was observed during careful normal-force movement.
```
