## 2. Objective

Verify that a previously paired phone reconnects to the headset after the headset is powered off and on again.

---

## 3. Preconditions

* TC-001 or equivalent pairing has already succeeded.
* The phone still stores the headset pairing record.
* Bluetooth remains enabled on the phone.
* Headset has enough battery for the test.

---

## 4. Test Data And Inputs

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Device under test | BRIDIO TH10 |
| Source device | Previously paired phone |
| Connection mode | Bluetooth |
| Delay before reconnect | 30 seconds |

---

## 5. Steps

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Confirm the headset is paired and connected. |
| 2 | Power off the headset. |
| 3 | Wait 30 seconds. |
| 4 | Power on the headset. |
| 5 | Observe connection state and play audio for 1 minute. |

---

## 6. Expected Result

The headset reconnects automatically or with a simple manual tap, and audio playback remains stable after reconnection.

---

## 7. Actual Result

```text
Recorded 2026-06-01 21:13 ICT. After the headset was powered off for 30 seconds and powered on again, the phone did not reconnect automatically within 60 seconds. Manual selection from Bluetooth settings was required. The first playback attempt after reconnection also had an initial mute period of about 2 seconds.
```
