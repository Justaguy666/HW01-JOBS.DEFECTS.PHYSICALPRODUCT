## 2. Objective

Check hinge robustness and audio/device stability under repeated rapid but normal open-close handling.

---

## 3. Preconditions

* Headset is physically inspected before the test.
* Tester uses safe normal force only.
* Audio playback can be checked after the mechanical cycles.

---

## 4. Test Data And Inputs

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Device under test | BRIDIO TH10 |
| Mechanical cycles | 20 open-close cycles |
| Mechanical speed | Faster than normal, but not forced |
| Post-check | Power on and audio playback |

---

## 5. Steps

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Inspect hinge condition before test. |
| 2 | Open and close the foldable hinge 20 times at a faster-than-normal but non-forced pace. |
| 3 | Check for looseness, clicking, cracks, or resistance. |
| 4 | Power on and play audio after the cycles. |
| 5 | Record any physical or functional change. |

---

## 6. Expected Result

The hinge should remain intact, movement should remain smooth, and audio should still work after rapid normal handling.

---

## 7. Actual Result

```text
Recorded 2026-06-01 22:32 ICT. After 20 rapid but non-forced open-close cycles, the right hinge developed a light clicking sound and felt looser than the left hinge. Audio still worked, but the hinge no longer felt symmetric during folding.
```
