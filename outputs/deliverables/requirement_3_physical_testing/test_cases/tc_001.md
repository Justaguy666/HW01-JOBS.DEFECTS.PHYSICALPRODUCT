## 2. Objective

Verify that the BRIDIO TH10 can enter pairing mode, appear on an Android phone, connect successfully, and play audio as the first-time wireless setup flow.

---

## 3. Preconditions

* BRIDIO TH10 is charged.
* Android phone Bluetooth is enabled.
* Previous TH10 pairing is removed if it exists.
* Phone and headset are within 1 meter at the start of the test.

---

## 4. Test Data And Inputs

| Input | Value |
| --- | --- |
| Device under test | BRIDIO TH10 |
| Source device | Android phone |
| Connection mode | Bluetooth |
| Starting condition | No active TH10 pairing on the phone |

---

## 5. Steps

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Power on the headset. |
| 2 | Open Android Bluetooth settings. |
| 3 | Search for available devices. |
| 4 | Select the TH10 or BRIDIO headset entry. |
| 5 | Play a short audio track after pairing. |

---

## 6. Expected Result

The headset appears in the Bluetooth device list, pairs successfully, shows connected status, and plays audio without obvious dropout at short range.
