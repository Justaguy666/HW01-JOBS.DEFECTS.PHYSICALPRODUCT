## 2. Objective

Check whether folding or pressure on only one side causes imbalance, discomfort, hinge looseness, or one-channel audio issues.

---

## 3. Preconditions

* Headset is physically intact before the test.
* Continuous audio playback is available.
* Tester uses normal one-side fold or pressure only.
* No excessive force is applied.

| Input | Value |

## 5. Steps

| Step | Action |
| --- | --- |
| 1 | Wear or hold the headset in normal orientation. |
| 2 | Apply normal folding pressure to only the left side and release. |
| 3 | Repeat on only the right side. |
| 4 | Play audio and compare left/right sound after each side action. |
| 5 | Inspect hinge alignment and wearing pressure balance. |

One-sided folding pressure should not create permanent imbalance, hinge misalignment, unusual discomfort, or left/right audio failure.
